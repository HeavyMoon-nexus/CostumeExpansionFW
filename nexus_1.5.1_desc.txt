1.5.1 Update — stability release!
The random CTD when adding to Persist is fixed at the root, and the "newest persist item never gets physics" problem is fixed too. CEF now ships a crash-defense net: if memory damage from any source reaches a CEF node, it is detected, repaired, quarantined and logged instead of crashing. Carrier rebuilds got dramatically faster and show progress notifications while they run. [b]First launch after updating rebuilds all carriers once[/b] — expected, one time only. If you ran a 1.5.1 test build, disable that test mod first. Full details in the changelog.


[size=5][b]Costume Expansion FW[/b][/size]

[b]Wear skinned accessories and costumes without spending biped slots.[/b] Skyrim has only 32 fixed equipment slots (30–61), and they run out fast. Costume Expansion FW (CEF) clones an item's skinned mesh and binds it directly to your skeleton, [i]bypassing the equip system[/i] — so several items can share one slot, and an accessory's original (often contested) slot is freed. It's effectively a slot-budget expansion.

[size=4][b]Two ways to show things[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Persist[/b] — always-on accessories (nails, piercings). No slot, no token. The persist list is a [b]catalog shared across saves[/b]; each save toggles what it actually shows, so a new character starts clean.[/*]
[*][b]Box[/b] — costumes gated by an invisible token. Equip the box's token to show its packed contents; a strip mod that removes the token hides them automatically. Several items can ride on one box token (one slot). Boxes exist for the custom slots (44–60) [b]and the vanilla slots[/b] (Head, Hair, Body, Hands, Feet, Amulet, Ring, Circlet, …) — a slot-31 box ([b]"Hair (Wig)"[/b]) masks your real hair while worn, a slot-32 box replaces what your body slot shows.[/*]
[/list]
Captured items keep their own tempering / player enchantment (stored in a hidden holding container). A worn box also passes through its contents' [b]armor, weight, armor class, enchantment effects, and keywords[/b], so the costume behaves like real gear.

[size=4][b]SMP cloth physics — fully automatic[/b][/size]
With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/57339]Faster HDT-SMP (FSMP)[/url] installed, [b]outfit-specific SMP cloth physics (skirts, veils, wigs) animates on injected content[/b]. CEF builds an invisible physics "carrier" from a box's contents, and since [b]1.2[/b] the carrier builder is [b]built into the plugin[/b]: change a box in the MCM and the carrier rebuilds itself in the background a couple of seconds later — no extra tool, no runtime, no setup, any mod manager. Then just [b]re-equip the box token until the outfit sways[/b]. Persist items get their physics fully automatically, no re-equip needed. Without FSMP (or for legacy NiTriShape meshes, which a validation gate excludes to prevent crashes) such parts render statically instead of vanishing.

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
Install with a mod manager (MO2/Vortex) and enable [b]CostumeFW.esp[/b] — a single ESL-flagged plugin (takes no load-order slot). With SKSE Menu Framework installed, CEF appears in its [b]Mod Control Panel[/b] (default [b]F1[/b]) automatically; with SkyUI, the legacy MCM registers too. Works on an existing save. That's all — carrier rebuilds are built into the plugin.

[b]Upgrading from 1.0.x:[/b] CostumeFW.esp replaces the old CostumeFW_Boxes.esp. Your box setups carry over automatically; the box tokens re-appear on their own (the old ones vanish with the old plugin), and previously captured originals are returned as fresh copies when you remove them from a box.

[size=4][b]How to use (SKSE Menu Framework → "Costume Expansion FW")[/b][/size]
Open the [b]Mod Control Panel[/b] (default [b]F1[/b]; gamepad: double-press LB, D-pad/A/B to navigate) and pick the [b]Costume Expansion FW[/b] section. The same pages exist in the legacy SkyUI MCM.
[list]
[*][b]Main[/b] — master on/off, dependency status, "Prepare for uninstall".[/*]
[*][b]Persist[/b] — "+ Add worn item" / "+ Add from inventory" capture an accessory (no need to equip it first; the Inventory filter input narrows the list by name). Per row: active-on-this-save toggle, Body morph toggle, Body (forced-gender NIF) menu, hide-when-worn slots, remove from catalog.[/*]
[*][b]Boxes[/b] — "+ New box" picks a free slot; per box: Distribute token, Wear (show/hide), capture items (same filter), Armor type, assign/export [b]Presets[/b], Stats, Delete; per item: remove / Body morph / Body (forced NIF) / hide-when-worn / [b]Hide shapes[/b] (drop a costume's bundled body shapes by name) / [b]Show real body[/b] (substitute your real morphed body under the garments — follows your active skin and its textures, so alternate-skin mods work).[/*]
[*][b]Presets[/b] — installed CEFP_*.json presets; assign one to a box or to Persist.[/*]
[*][b]Diagnostics[/b] — dependency status, live carrier-sync status (stage / step / elapsed while a rebuild runs), the physics-bone budget (heaviest shape vs the per-shape 80, FSMP merge asked-vs-granted), carrier revisions, persist registration, churn counters, and a verbose debug-log toggle.[/*]
[/list]
Presets (Data\SKSE\Plugins\CEF\Presets\CEFP_*.json) are the shareable unit — a named set of content you can hand out. Settings (Data\SKSE\Plugins\CEF_settings.json) are a catalog shared across saves; which persist entries actually show is stored per-save.

[b]About the legacy MCM:[/b] the SkyUI MCM is feature-frozen and kept only for the transition. In the next feature update it will shrink to a migration notice, and it will be removed in the version after that — freeing one of SkyUI's 128 MCM slots for older mods that will never update. Both UIs read and write the same settings, so you can switch at any time.

Known limitation: items are tracked by their base form — two individuals of the same item (e.g. differently enchanted copies) cannot be captured separately; the second is rejected as a duplicate. The captured copy's player-enchant effects are snapshotted and applied.

[size=4][b]Notes & limitations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Body morph[/b] (RaceMenu sliders) is per-content [b]opt-in, default OFF[/b] — turn it on only for BodySlide body-conforming meshes. Keep it OFF for hair/jewelry/accessories (wasted work and memory there).[/*]
[*]Body physics (CBPC / standard-bone SMP) works out of the box on injected meshes.[/*]
[*]After changing a box's contents, [b]re-equip the box token until the outfit sways[/b] — physics carriers apply on equip, by design.[/*]
[*]Full wigs belong in the slot-31 wig box, [b]not[/b] in Persist. — However, I have made it work to some extent with `persist` as well. Please note that there is no guarantee of proper operation, and memory usage may increase. Since existing hair is not removed, the hair will appear overlapped.[/*]
[*]Content must be weighted to bones on your live skeleton (standard XPMSSE). Content built for a different/extended skeleton may show parts statically.[/*]
[*][b]Alternate-skin mods[/b] (RaceMenu Selector of Skins, Unique Player-style setups, transformation/custom-race skins): compatible. "Show real body" follows your active skin — mesh [i]and[/i] texture set — the same way the engine resolves it.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Reporting problems[/b][/size]
CEF keeps an always-on flight recorder sized for bug reports: an environment census at startup, a roll call of every registration after loading (failures are named, not silent), and a mirror of your settings written right next to the log. If something goes wrong, attach these two files (plus your crash log, if any):
[list]
[*]Documents\My Games\Skyrim Special Edition\SKSE\[b]CostumeExpansionFW.log[/b][/*]
[*]same folder, [b]CEF_settings.mirror.json[/b][/*]
[/list]
For a problem that keeps coming back, create Data\SKSE\Plugins\[b]CostumeExpansionFW.ini[/b] (if you don't have one) containing a [b][Diagnostics][/b] section with [b]bDebugMode=1[/b] — or tick the checkbox on the Diagnostics page for one session — then just play normally until it happens again and send the same files. The verbose log usually pins it down. Debug mode changes logging only, never behavior; set it back to 0 afterwards, the log grows large.

[size=4][b]Uninstalling[/b][/size]
Open [b]Main → Prepare for uninstall[/b] first (in the SKSE Menu Framework panel or the legacy MCM — returns captured items, removes the tokens, detaches the meshes, and disables CEF persistently), save, then remove the mod. Box definitions and the persist catalog are kept in CEF_settings.json, so re-enabling (or reinstalling) restores your setup.
